Surface plasmon resonance immunosensor for ErbB2 breast cancer biomarker 
determination in human serum and raw cancer cell lysates.

Eletxigerra U(1), Martinez-Perdiguero J(2), Barderas R(3), Pingarrón JM(4), 
Campuzano S(4), Merino S(1).

A highly sensitive surface plasmon resonance (SPR) immunosensor for the 
important ErbB2 breast cancer biomarker has been developed. Optimization of the 
assay has been carried out, including signal enhancement employing gold 
nanoparticles (GNPs). The effect of the signal amplification of the GNPs has 
been also studied. The assay has been tested with clinically relevant matrices. 
Results in 50% human serum yielded a LOD of 180 pg mL(-1) which is a 
concentration 83 times lower than the clinical cut-off. Raw lysates from model 
breast cancer cell lines (SK-BR-3, MCF-7 and MDA-MB-436) have been also assayed 
and higher quantities of the ErbB2 protein were clearly observed in the ErbB2 
over-expressing case (SK-BR-3). The results confirmed that the simple and highly 
sensitive SPR immunosensor represents a feasible tool for ErbB2 detection.
