Claim: In the phase 3, open-label, international DESTINY-Breast05 trial (NCT04622319), 1,635 patients with HER2-positive breast cancer and residual invasive disease after neoadjuvant therapy were randomized 1:1 to trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd; 818 patients) or trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1; 817 patients). At approximately 30 months' median follow-up, invasive-disease events or deaths occurred in 51/818 (6.2%) patients receiving T-DXd versus 102/817 (12.5%) receiving T-DM1, giving an invasive disease-free survival hazard ratio of 0.47 (95% CI, 0.34-0.66; P<0.001); 3-year invasive disease-free survival was 92.4% versus 83.7%. Adjudicated drug-related interstitial lung disease occurred in 9.6% versus 1.6%, with two ILD-related deaths in the T-DXd group.
