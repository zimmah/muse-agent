Compassionate use of humanized anti-HER2/neu protein, trastuzumab for metastatic 
breast cancer in Japan.

Tokuda Y(1), Suzuki Y, Ohta M, Saito Y, Kubota M, Tajima T, Umemura S, Osamura 
RY.

Author information:
(1)Department of Surgery, Tokai University School of Medicine, Bohseidai, 
Isehara, Kanagawa 259-1193, Japan. tokuda@is.icc.u-tokai.ac.jp

The HER-2/neu protein is thought to be a unique and useful target for antibody 
therapy of cancers overexpressing the HER-2/neu gene. The recombinant humanized 
anti-HER-2 monoclonal antibody, trastuzumab (Herceptin) was approved for 
clinical use in the US in 1998. In Japan, it was approved and later became 
available in June, 2001. We have treated 41 patients with metastatic breast 
cancer with trastuzumab purchased from the US. In this paper, the details of the 
patients we experienced are reviewed.
