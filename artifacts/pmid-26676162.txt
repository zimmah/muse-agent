T-DM1 Extends Survival in HER2+ Breast Cancer.

[No authors listed]

Results from the phase III TH3RESA study show that T-DM1 is effective in 
patients with metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer that has progressed despite 
two or more HER2-targeted therapies, extending their median overall survival by 
nearly 7 months.
