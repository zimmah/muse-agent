Trastuzumab emtansine: determining its role in management of HER2+ breast 
cancer.

Doroshow DB(1), LoRusso PM(1).

Author information:
(1)Yale Cancer Center, PO Box 208028, New Haven, CT 06510, USA.

Trastuzumab emtansine is an antibody-drug conjugate comprised of the anti-HER2 
monoclonal antibody trastuzumab linked to DM1 (emtansine), a potent cytotoxic 
maytansinoid derivative, by a stable linker. This structure results in improved 
tumor-directed cytotoxicity in HER2+ breast cancer with reduced systemic 
toxicities, particularly the cardiac toxicities associated with single agent 
trastuzumab. Phase III trials have demonstrated improved progression-free and 
overall survival in heavily pretreated patients with advanced HER2+ breast 
cancer, with an acceptable toxicity profile. However, its role in first-line 
treatment is less clear. Ongoing studies continue to evaluate its role in 
neoadjuvant and adjuvant management of HER2+ breast cancer.
